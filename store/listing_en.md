## Full description (max 4000 characters)

When did you last get a haircut? Change the sheets? Call your mum?

Kaç Gün Oldu? ("How many days has it been?") shows at a glance how long it has been since the small but important things in your life. It doesn't tell you what to do like a calendar; it remembers when you did it and tells you how far you are from your usual rhythm.

HOW IT WORKS
• Add what you want to track as a card: "Went to the dentist", "Watered the plants", "Went to the gym"…
• When you do it, tap the card and press "Bugün yaptım" (done today). Forgot? Pick a past day instead.
• Every card shows how many days it has been and when it is due: "3 days left", "Due today", "8 days late".

SMART RHYTHM
After a few records the app learns your rhythm by itself. You can also choose "weekly", "monthly" or any interval of your own. The card's colour tells its state; overdue cards always stand out.

REMINDERS
Turn reminders on for the cards you choose and get a notification at your chosen time when one comes due. Reminders are scheduled entirely on your device.

FEATURES
• Grid or list view, drag to reorder
• Overdue page: swipe right to mark as done today
• Timeline: every record, month by month
• Card history: delete a record or change its date, every step undoable
• Profile and statistics: this month at a glance, your most regular habit, the one you have put off longest
• 6 colour themes, including a dark "Gece" theme
• Copy a backup to the clipboard, restore it on another device

PRIVACY
No account, no ads, no internet connection. Your cards and records stay on your phone only; nothing is collected, nothing is sent anywhere.

The app's interface is in Turkish.
